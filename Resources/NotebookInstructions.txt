Part 3 — Classify it with a language model (25 points)

3.1 Bare — "Is this movie review positive or negative?" plus the review.

3.2 Constrained — specify the exact labels and the output format you want. "Answer with exactly one word: POSITIVE or NEGATIVE."

3.3 Few-shot — include two or three labelled examples in the prompt before the review you want classified.

This is what prompt engineering is: not phrasing tricks, but a measurable change in behaviour that you can demonstrate with numbers.
